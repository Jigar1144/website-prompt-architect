


# V5 Production Pipeline

```text
USER
  ↓
4 USER-FACING INTERVIEW ROUNDS
  ↓
MASTER CREATIVE + CONVERSION BRIEF
  ↓
BUSINESS DIFFERENTIATION
  ↓
CONVERSION + OBJECTION MAP
  ↓
PAGE ARCHITECTURE ENGINE
  ↓
COMPONENT ARCHITECTURE
  ↓
DESIGN SYSTEM
  ↓
CONTENT TRUST GATE
  ↓
IMAGE ART DIRECTION
  ↓
SEO / LOCAL SEO
  ↓
INTELLIGENT TECHNOLOGY SELECTION
  ↓
MOTION / 3D GATE
  ↓
ANALYTICS PLAN
  ↓
AI CODING-AGENT HANDOFF
  ↓
QA / LAUNCH GATE
```

## Core invariant

Every generated prompt must consume the same approved MASTER CREATIVE + CONVERSION BRIEF. Do not independently reinterpret the business in downstream prompts.

## Decision hierarchy

1. Explicit user decisions
2. Verified business facts
3. Approved strategic inferences
4. Industry best practices
5. Creative defaults

Lower-priority assumptions must never override higher-priority information.

## Output quality test

Before finalizing, verify:
- sitemap has a reason for every page
- components are reusable but not over-generalized
- technology is justified
- factual claims are gated
- images have art direction
- analytics has a primary conversion
- coding-agent handoff is implementation-ready
- design complexity is justified
