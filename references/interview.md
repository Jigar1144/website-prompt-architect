# Guided Interview — Four User-Facing Rounds

The user-facing interview is capped at **4 bundled rounds**. The system still
runs a deeper internal decision engine with 10+ phases. Never expose the internal
ladder as a long questionnaire.

## Round 1 — Business + outcome
**Ask:** “What is the business/brand name, what does it do, who is it for, and what is the main thing you want the website to achieve?”

Capture business, audience, offer, primary/secondary goals, visitor intent, and trust model.

## Round 2 — Differentiation + conversion
**Ask:** “Why should someone choose this business over alternatives, and what might stop them from taking the next step?”

Capture positioning, differentiator, objections, friction, desired CTA, and proof requirements.

Translate objections into design responses. Never invent claims or proof.

## Round 3 — Direction + market/SEO + assets
**Ask:** “What brand/assets already exist, where does the business operate or sell, how should the website feel, and are there any references you like?”

Capture brand/assets, market, search intent, local SEO needs, visual taste, motion tolerance, and references.

## Round 4 — Features + real-world constraints
**Ask:** “What must the website do, what must be preserved or avoided, and do you have any technology, maintenance, timeline, content, accessibility, or integration constraints?”

Capture features, CMS needs, maintenance owner, migration/SEO preservation, integrations, timeline, performance, accessibility, legal/compliance, and hard constraints.

## Trust/proof inventory
Mark each as Available / Partial / Missing / Must not claim:
- reviews/testimonials
- certifications/licenses
- awards
- clients/partners
- case studies
- before/after evidence
- credentials/team expertise
- guarantees/policies
- media mentions
- measurable outcomes

## SEO/local SEO layer
Infer or capture search intent, service/product keywords, location modifiers,
service areas, map/contact prominence, local business schema opportunities,
service/location page needs, and internal linking priorities.

## Over-design gate
If an effect does not improve comprehension, hierarchy, emotional positioning,
understanding, trust, or conversion, remove it. Score complexity Low/Medium/High/
Extreme and justify High/Extreme choices.

## Responsive interview rule
Do not ask users about breakpoints or advanced responsive implementation details.
Infer responsive behavior from the approved direction and stack.

## Final source of truth
Compile one internal brief before downstream prompts. Do not re-ask known fields.
